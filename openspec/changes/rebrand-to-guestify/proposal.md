## Why

The product is being renamed from **Visimio** to **Guestify**, with a new
logo: a teal location-pin "G" mark, and a wordmark with "Gu" in teal and
"estify" in white. Every sidebar, header, footer, the favicon, the tab
title and the push-notification fallback still say Visimio. The API's
emails are rebranded by the paired `VMSS_API` change `rebrand-to-guestify`.
That change also builds the SVG logo master and generates this repo's brand assets with
`scripts/build_brand_assets.py --web-out`, so it lands first.

## What Changes

- **New brand assets, vector.** The API change rebuilds the supplied
  raster logo as a high-quality SVG master (`VMSS_API/brand/`). Its
  script copies or renders these into this repo:
  - `src/assets/brand/logo-guestify.svg`: light lockup, with "estify" in
    dark navy for white surfaces;
  - `logo-guestify-on-dark.svg`: white "estify", for the navy sidebars and
    staff headers;
  - `logo-guestify-mark.svg`: the "G" pin mark alone;
  - `public/favicon.svg`, plus PNG fallbacks `favicon.png`, `favicon-32.png`
    and `apple-touch-icon.png`.

  The logos are now SVG instead of 1×/2× PNG pairs, so they stay sharp at
  any size and pixel density, and `srcSet` is no longer needed. The six
  `logo-visimio*.png` files are deleted.
- **`src/presentation/brand.ts`:** `BRAND_NAME = 'Guestify'`, with one
  SVG import per variant. `BrandLogo` drops `srcSet`. Because every surface already reads from this
  module through `<BrandLogo>`, the sidebars, mobile bar, portal
  header/footer, staff and SSO login headers/footers, and copyright lines
  all change without per-component edits.
- **Literals that bypass `brand.ts`:**
  - `index.html`: `<title>Guestify</title>`, plus a
    `<link rel="icon" type="image/svg+xml" href="/favicon.svg">` ahead of
    the PNG favicons;
  - `public/firebase-messaging-sw.js` fallback title "Guestify";
  - the `aria-label="Visimio home"` in `PortalHeader` and `SidebarHeader`
    becomes `` `${BRAND_NAME} home` ``;
  - the `BrandLogo` comment;
  - `e2e/test_toasts.py`.
- **Layout re-check.** The Guestify lockup is wider than Visimio's (about
  3.1:1). Each logo slot is checked at phone and desktop widths, so the
  wider wordmark doesn't crowd the sidebar header, mobile bar or staff
  header badge.
- **`scripts/check-brand.mjs`** now also fails on "Visimio" (case-insensitive)
  in shipped code, not just "VisitorPass".
- **Support contact.** The portal footer still shows
  `support@visitorpass.co`, from the older VisitorPass brand. It becomes a
  `VITE_SUPPORT_EMAIL` setting (added to `.env.example`). When the setting
  is unset, the line is hidden, so no made-up address ships. The brand
  guard then also checks "visitorpass" case-insensitively, so the old
  domain can't come back.

Out of scope:
- internal identifiers (package name, env var names, repo names);
- tenant company logos;
- the portal colour theme: the logo's teal and navy already sit within
  the current palette.

### Modified Capabilities
- `brand-identity`: the product name, logo, alt text, home-link name,
  favicon, tab title, push title and copyright line are "Guestify". The
  retired-name rule now covers "Visimio" too. The footer-logo size scenario
  reflects the new aspect ratio. Its "Logo Variant Matches Its Background"
  requirement is carried by `modernize-visitor-flow-pages` instead, since
  public pages no longer have a light header for the dark-wordmark logo.
- `staff-login`: no delta here any more. `modernize-login-pages` replaced the
  dark header with a brand panel that already names the Guestify logo, and
  its sync carries that requirement.
- `visitor-portal-landing`: no delta here any more.
  `modernize-visitor-landing-page` carries the Guestify identity and the
  configured support contact (hidden when unset) in its own delta.

## Impact

- Assets: 6 PNGs in `src/assets/brand/` become 3 SVGs, `public/` gains
  `favicon.svg`, and its 3 PNGs are re-rendered. All of them are
  generated by `VMSS_API/scripts/build_brand_assets.py --web-out ../VMMS_WEB`.
  There's no new web dependency, since Vite imports `.svg` as a URL out of
  the box.
- Code: `src/presentation/brand.ts`, `components/BrandLogo.tsx` (drop `srcSet`),
  `components/PortalHeader.tsx`, `components/sidebar/SidebarHeader.tsx`,
  `index.html`, `public/firebase-messaging-sw.js`, `scripts/check-brand.mjs`
  and `e2e/test_toasts.py`.
- `index.html` already has uncommitted local edits on this branch. Only the
  `<title>` line and the favicon `<link>` lines are touched, and the
  existing edits are left alone.
- No API or routing change. Bundle size changes by a few KB.
